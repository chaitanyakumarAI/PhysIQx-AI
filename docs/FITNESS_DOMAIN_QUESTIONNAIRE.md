## SECTION 1 — Body Composition & Visual Body Types

> *Feeds into: BodyShapeStep, Body Shape pillar scoring, body shape images*

1. What are the most scientifically accepted visual categories for human body
   composition types used in fitness assessment? Are the classic 3 somatotypes
   (ectomorph/mesomorph/endomorph) still considered valid by sports science, or
   have they been replaced?

2. What specific **visual characteristics** (proportions, fat distribution, muscle
   insertion points, bone structure) clearly distinguish a "lean" body from an
   "athletic" body from a "muscular" body at a glance — and how would a trained
   eye describe each one to a layperson?

3. What body fat percentage ranges correspond to each body type for men and
   women? (e.g., Lean = 8–13% for men, Athletic = 13–20%?)

4. How does body composition visually differ between **male and female** bodies
   of the same training level? What are the most misunderstood differences?

5. What are the most common **starting body types** for people who begin a fitness
   app journey — what does the "average untrained person" look like by gender, age
   group, and region?

6. When someone selects a goal body shape, how long does it realistically take
   to achieve it from an average starting point? What's the science behind
   body recomposition timelines?

9. What is a realistic rate of strength gain for a beginner, intermediate, and

## SECTION 3 — Cardiovascular Fitness Science

14. What cardio metrics can be tracked **without a wearable** (heart rate monitor)?

    cardiovascular improvements, and how does this change by experience level?

17. How do strength training sessions affect cardiovascular fitness, and vice versa?
